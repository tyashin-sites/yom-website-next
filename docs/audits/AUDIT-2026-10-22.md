- Own identity: paper/ink/vermilion/cobalt/brass palette, Instrument Serif + Schibsted Grotesk + Geist Mono, null-point mark. No purple, no teal/pink, no Thridify choreography (no GSAP, no magnetic CTAs, no spotlight, no metric band, no marquee).

- Every "Coming soon" item is from the brief's roadmap list and labelled.
